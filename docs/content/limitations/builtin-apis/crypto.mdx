- **Crypto:** Hashing, HMAC, PBKDF2, and HKDF support every digest `crypto.getHashes()` lists in Node 24, with OpenSSL's aliases and object identifiers.
  Hash and Hmac accept every Buffer encoding and any TypedArray or DataView, `createHash` and `hash.copy` take `outputLength`, and `crypto.hash` takes its options object; HMAC, PBKDF2, and HKDF reject the SHAKE and KMAC digests with Node's errors.
  Hash and Hmac values are also Transforms: they can be written, piped into, used as `pipeline()` stages, read, and listened to, and they report Node's errors after the stream flushes.
  `copy()` of a Hash whose stream flushed, and stream use of a value after its stream closed, throw a coded SC2020 error.
  Through `unknown` and `any` slots and untyped JavaScript, Hash and Hmac values keep their identity and support `update()`, `digest()`, and Hash's `copy()`, called or read as values; their stream and event members there throw a coded SC2020 error.
  `timingSafeEqual` compares any view or ArrayBuffer.
  Secret KeyObjects (`createSecretKey`, `generateKey` and `generateKeySync` for `hmac` and `aes`) support `type`, `symmetricKeySize`, `export()`, `equals()`, and `instanceof KeyObject`, and createHmac, hkdf, createCipheriv, and createDecipheriv accept them; asymmetric keys, `KeyObject.from()`, and `toCryptoKey()` are not available.
  `randomFill`, `randomFillSync` (every view kind and ArrayBuffer), `randomInt`, `randomUUID`, and `randomUUIDv7` follow Node 24.22, including callback timing; a `randomInt` callback whose error parameter is typed `Error | null` receives `null` where Node passes `undefined`.
  Scrypt supports its default parameters, numeric options, aliases, and memory limit.
  Both KDFs accept strings, Buffers, numeric typed arrays, DataViews, and fixed-length ArrayBuffers.
  `createCipheriv` and `createDecipheriv` support aes-128, aes-192, and aes-256 in CBC, CTR, and GCM modes and chacha20-poly1305, on native targets, through `update()`, `final()`, `setAAD()`, `setAutoPadding()`, `getAuthTag()`, and `setAuthTag()`; other ciphers, cipher streams, and wasm32-wasi builds are refused.
  Decrypting chacha20-poly1305 without an authentication tag fails in `final()`; Node 24.22 returns unverified plaintext.
  After `update()` fails, for example past the AES-GCM limit of 2^36 - 32 bytes per message, every later call on that cipher throws; Node still lets `final()` run.
  An output encoding that is a function or an instance of a class defining `toString` throws a coded SC2020 error.
  `createECDH` and `ECDH.convertKey` support prime192v1, secp224r1, prime256v1, secp384r1, secp521r1, secp192k1, secp224k1, secp256k1, brainpoolP256r1, brainpoolP384r1, and brainpoolP512r1, on native targets, through `generateKeys()`, `computeSecret()`, `getPublicKey()`, `getPrivateKey()`, `setPrivateKey()`, and `setPublicKey()`, in compressed, uncompressed, and hybrid forms; the other curves `crypto.getCurves()` lists and wasm32-wasi builds are refused.
  A call on an ECDH value whose arguments no @types/node overload accepts (`getPublicKey("HEX")`) is refused at compile time, as is `computeSecret(view, encoding)`: Node reads that encoding as the input encoding and answers a Buffer where @types/node declares a string.
  ECDH property reads and writes throw a coded SC2020 error.
  Other public-key operations remain unsupported.
  Native derivations finish before their deferred callbacks, so computation blocks the caller.
